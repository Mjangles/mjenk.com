Zipster Rewards grew customer value 78% in six months.

Launching Zippy's first digital loyalty program and app, and moving a devoted, older customer base onto it without leaving anyone behind.

Director, Loyalty & Customer Insight2021–2024Honolulu, HI
+78%customer value in six months
~15%of Hawaiʻi signed up in two years

The challenge

Zippy's needed to replace its Senior Club with a digital rewards program. Our kupuna customers made Zippy's what it is, so the program had to keep them. It also had to start a relationship with the next generation of Zippy's fans.

The insight

Any change needs two things: ease and motivation. Our longest-standing customers needed a hand to hold through the switch. Younger customers needed a brand that felt modern and celebrated them, not just the food.

What I did

  1. Kupuna firstWe started with direct mail to members of the Senior Club, so our longest-standing customers were brought along step by step.
  2. A brand that celebrates customersA modern, youthful look for Zipster Rewards that reflected the community and put customers ahead of the food.
  3. Make it easyBrochures and video tutorials gave every customer the help they needed to sign up.
  4. Make it worth itFor a limited time, a free Zip Pac after the first purchase as a Zipster. It was a significant offer, and it drove a highly successful launch.
  5. Personalize at scaleA digital promotions engine built on behavioral segmentation, with automated offer delivery.

The outcome

Customer value rose 78% in the first six months. After two years, nearly 15% of the state's population had signed up. The data and insight from the program became a key input to Zippy's strategy, including menu planning, operational investments and expansion.
