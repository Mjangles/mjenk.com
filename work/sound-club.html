The idea

Albums have connected artists and listeners for generations, but playlists and algorithms are boxing them in. Sound Club is a place to celebrate the album: clubs that listen to one record at a time, rate it and talk it through, online and in person. The tagline says it: discover albums through people, not algorithms.

Why it matters for a marketer

Sound Club is proof that AI changes what one marketer can do with an idea. I was able to build it from concept to community with the help of Claude Code.

What I did with AI

  1. Branding & positioningTook the concept of book clubs for music and worked through branding, positioning and a messaging architecture built around the shared experience of an album.
  2. ProductBuilt a website and iOS and Android apps that bring people together in listening clubs, where they listen to, rate and discuss albums.
  3. Launch operationsApp Store and Google Play submissions, privacy and permissions, analytics and data migrations across multiple builds.
  4. Paid mediaRan acquisition campaigns with creative tests that cut sign-up costs 49% over the first six months.
  5. Ongoing optimization & maintenanceKeep the product roadmap current through ongoing surveys and one-on-one research, and fix bugs and ship improvements with Claude Code.
